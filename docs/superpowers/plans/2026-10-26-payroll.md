## Decisions the spec left open

- **Employment type lives on each pay rate** (spec 8.1). `employees.employment_type` stays as the default type when adding a rate.
- **Closing is two database steps around the TypeScript calculation.** `start_payroll_close` checks blocking shifts and sets the period to `closing`, which already locks the month; the route computes payslips with `calculatePayslip`; `finish_payroll_close` stores the version and payslips and sets `closed` in one transaction. Any failure calls `cancel_payroll_close`. This keeps one calculation for estimates and for closing (spec 11.4) without a race with shift edits.
- **The month lock is a trigger** on `shifts`, `monthly_configs` (update/delete) and `pay_adjustments`, so it also guards the service role. It takes `FOR SHARE` on the period row so a write waits for a close in progress.
- **A month can be closed only after it ends.** This keeps check in, which always writes today's date, from ever hitting a closed month.
- **Minutes are whole minutes** (floor of the shift length and of its overtime part). Every money line is rounded to the dong before summing (spec 8.6).
- **Full time shifts earn nothing extra**, overtime included; their hours still appear per branch.
- **A shift with no pay rate on its day earns nothing** and the payslip says how many such shifts there are.
- **Pay rates can be added or deleted, not edited.** Closed months keep their stored payslips either way.
- **"Lương của tôi" is in the nav for every role except admin and director**, who use the payroll board.
- **Managers see pay only with the `payroll.view` permission**, a change from spec 4.2 asked for by the owner (`supabase/migrations/20261026120000_manager_payroll_view.sql`). They read the board, history and export of everyone; closing, reopening, adjustments, pay rates and config stay with admins and directors. To compute estimates they also read every valid shift, not only those of their branches.
